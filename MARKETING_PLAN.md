# Getting Retailers to Your Godown Without a Storefront

*A working plan for **Shakeel Mobile Accessories** — Hasan Center, Hall Road, Lahore. Built around zero marketing budget, solo time, 300 dormant WhatsApp contacts, and no walk-in traffic. Written 11 Aug 2026.*

**Situation:** 300 contacts (dormant 30+ days) · 0 walk-in traffic at the godown · Rs. 0 marketing budget

Here's the actual shift: you didn't lose a marketing channel when you lost the shop — a shop window was never how wholesale buyers found you anyway. Retailers who travel from other cities to buy in bulk choose a supplier on **trust, price, and being easy to reorder from**, not foot traffic. That means the fix isn't "replace the shop," it's building the three things that actually drive wholesale reorders: a warm list that hears from you regularly, enough public visibility that new retailers can find you cold, and a reason for existing buyers to bring the next one.

---

## Phase 1 — This week (cost: Rs. 0)

### 1.1 Relaunch the WhatsApp group — don't just rename it

A group that's been quiet a month and suddenly gets renamed with no explanation reads as suspicious to 300 people who half-remember a partner dispute. Announce the change before you rename anything, then rename, then re-engage in three short messages spaced a few days apart — this "miss you → value → last call" pattern is the standard way to win back a dormant list, and it reliably recovers 10–15% of an inactive group on its own.

**Message 1 · relaunch** — *sent the day you rename the group*
> Assalam o Alaikum. Kuch tabdeeliyon ke baad ab yeh group naye naam **Shakeel Mobile Accessories** se chal raha hai — wahi aap ka purana wholesale contact, Hasan Center, Hall Road se. Neeche is hafte ka stock list hai, rates attached hain. Order ke liye direct reply karein.

**Message 2 · value** — *3–4 days later, only to those who didn't reply to message 1*
> New stock aa gaya hai — [1–2 items]. Group members ke liye is hafte free delivery / [small discount] hai first reorder par. List attached.

**Message 3 · last call** — *~2 weeks later, only to the still-silent remainder, then stop*
> Agar ab active nahi rehna chahte to koi masla nahi — bas bata dein, list bhejna band kar dete hain. Warna reply karein, latest rates bhej dete hain.

Stop after message 3 for anyone who doesn't respond — chasing further just trains people to ignore you. The ones who do respond are your live list; that's worth more than 300 silent numbers.

### 1.2 Split "the group" into a Channel and a Broadcast list

Right now you have one blunt instrument. WhatsApp Business actually gives you three, and using the right one for each job is free:

- **Broadcast list** — for your existing 300 (already saved your number). Messages land as a normal private chat, not a group post, which is why broadcast messages see ~98% open rates versus a group post people scroll past. Limit is 256 per list, so split your contacts into two lists. Use this for price-list drops and direct offers.
- **WhatsApp Channel** — a one-way public feed, unlimited followers, no reply clutter. Anyone can follow via a link without saving your number first — good for the retailer who found you on Facebook or TikTok but isn't ready to DM yet. Use it for stock-arrival photos, price updates, general announcements.
- **The group itself** — keep it, but for retailers talking to each other (bulk-order coordination, questions), not as your main megaphone.

**The one requirement that trips people up:** a broadcast list only delivers to someone if they're saved in *your* phone's contacts *and* they have *your* number saved too. If either side is missing, the message silently doesn't arrive — no error. Since your 300 are old group members, most who've actually ordered before likely already have you saved, but don't assume all 300 do. This is exactly why the Channel matters — it works via a shareable link and sidesteps this requirement entirely.

**Step 1 — get the 300 numbers into your phone contacts.** WhatsApp won't bulk-export a group's members into contacts, so it has to be done per number: open the group → group name → **Group info** → **Participants** → tap each name showing only a number → **Add to contacts**, saved with a name that tells you what they are (e.g. `WS - Faisalabad - Ahmed Mobile`). Do 20–30 a day over the week rather than all 300 at once, prioritizing anyone you recognize as a repeat past buyer.

**Step 2 — create two Broadcast Lists** (300 contacts ÷ 256 cap ≈ two lists, split by city/region so you can later target a region-specific offer):
- *Android:* Chats tab → **⋮** menu (top right) → **New broadcast** → select contacts → tap ✔ to create.
- *iPhone:* Chats tab → **Broadcast Lists** → **New List** → add contacts → **Create**.
- Name them clearly, e.g. `Broadcast - Lahore/Central` and `Broadcast - Other Cities`. To use one, open it like any chat and send your price list/photos — each recipient gets it as a private 1-to-1 message, and replies come back to you individually.

**Step 3 — create the WhatsApp Channel:**
- *Android:* **Updates** tab → **+** → **New channel** → **Continue**.
- *iPhone:* **Updates** tab → **+** icon → **Create channel**.
- Name it **Shakeel Mobile Accessories — Wholesale Updates**, add a one-line description (what you sell, that you're wholesale-only, your rough MOQ), add a simple icon, then **Create**. Open **Channel info** → **Invite link** and copy it — paste this into the WhatsApp group, your Facebook Page/posts, your TikTok/Instagram bio, and your Google Business Profile description.

**Step 4 — repurpose the existing group:** rename it to **Shakeel Mobile Accessories** (this is message 1 above), edit the group description to one line — *"For quick price/stock updates, follow our Channel: [link]. This group is for questions and order coordination."* — and pin it. From here on, only use the group for things that genuinely need replies (order questions, availability checks); the full price list goes to the Broadcast lists instead.

| Content | Where it goes |
|---|---|
| New stock / price list update | Both Broadcast lists |
| General announcement, stock photos, "who we are" video | Channel |
| A retailer's specific question about an order | Group, or a direct reply |
| A new contact met at a market / referred by someone | Send the Channel invite link first (low commitment); add to a Broadcast list once they've ordered once |

### 1.3 Build the WhatsApp Business catalog

Free, built into the app, holds up to 500 items — image, price, and an item code per entry. Since your own inventory already carries a SKU per item, this is a straight copy job, not new work: photograph each item once against a plain background, list SKU + price, and you have a proper digital price list buyers can scroll without you typing it out over chat every time someone asks "what's your rate on X." This same catalog later plugs into a Facebook Shop at no extra effort (Phase 2).

### 1.4 Claim a Google Business Profile — as a service-area business, not a storefront

This is the free channel most wholesalers in your position skip, and it's the one that gets you found by a retailer who doesn't know you exist yet and just searches "mobile accessories wholesale Lahore." Register as a **service-area business**: Google's own rules for this category say to *not* list an unstaffed warehouse address — you hide the Hasan Center address entirely and instead list Lahore plus the other cities your buyers already travel from as your service area. You get found in search and maps without ever implying there's a shopfront to walk into.

## Phase 2 — This month (cost: Rs. 0)

### 2.1 List on the free B2B directories

These exist specifically so a retailer can find a wholesaler without visiting every market shop by shop — exactly your situation now. Free tier is enough to start; don't pay for premium placement until you've seen an inquiry actually come from one.

| Platform | What it's for |
|---|---|
| Bayopari | Pakistan-focused B2B marketplace for wholesalers/traders — general product listing |
| TradeWheel | Pakistan B2B supplier directory, good for a public company/product profile |
| TradeKey | Larger B2B marketplace, useful if you'd ever sell beyond Pakistan too |
| B2BMAP | Pakistan trade portal/directory — another free surface for the same listing |

Reuse the same product photos and copy from your WhatsApp catalog for all four — don't rewrite it per site.

### 2.2 Show up where mobile-accessory retailers already gather

There are existing Facebook groups built around exactly this trade — "Pakistan Mobiles Accessories Wholesale Dealer" and several Hall Road / Hassan Center-specific groups and pages. Join them, post stock updates with real photos on a regular schedule, and answer price questions in comments — that visibility compounds. Then create your own Facebook Page and connect it to the same catalog you built in 1.3, so it doubles as a Facebook Shop for free.

### 2.3 Short-form video — the highest-reach free channel available right now

Other Hall Road / Hasan Center sellers are already posting TikTok content showing their shops and stock — this isn't speculative for your market, it's already working for direct competitors. Organic reach on TikTok and Instagram Reels in Pakistan is currently cheap and doesn't need a following to start getting views. You don't need production value — a phone, good light, and honesty about being a real wholesale stock source is the content:

- New carton/shipment arriving — open it on camera, show quantity and packaging
- A fast price-comparison of 2–3 fast-moving items vs. retail
- A simple "who we are now" video introducing **Shakeel Mobile Accessories** and that you're still the same supplier as before, just under the new name

Two short videos a week is a realistic, sustainable pace solo — consistency matters more than frequency here.

## Ongoing — habits, not one-time tasks

### 3.1 Ask for referrals directly — retailers already talk to other retailers

Referral marketing is consistently one of the strongest B2B acquisition channels precisely because a retailer trusts another retailer's supplier recommendation more than any ad. Your 300 contacts already know retailers in their own cities you've never reached. Don't wait for it to happen organically — ask explicitly, and reward it: "refer a shop that places a first order, both of you get [small discount / free delivery]" costs you nothing until a real order lands.

### 3.2 Replace "come see the shop" trust with remote-order trust

Buyers from other cities were never inspecting your old shop in person for most of the relationship either — but now there's no shop to even mention. Rebuild that confidence explicitly: honest photos/video of actual current stock (not stock photography), a clear stated return/replacement policy, and offering a video call to show real inventory before a new buyer's first large order. This is what makes a stranger comfortable sending an advance payment to someone in another city.

### 3.3 Reply fast, and note where each new buyer came from

In wholesale, the supplier who answers the price question first often gets the order — slow replies lose deals you never hear about. Also worth a habit, not a system: when a new customer places their first order, jot down in one line how they found you (referral / Facebook group / TikTok / Google / directory). After a couple of months you'll know which of the channels above is actually pulling weight and can stop spending time on the ones that aren't.

> **Avoid:** buying bulk contact-number lists to blast — WhatsApp requires recipients to have opted in, and mass unsolicited messaging gets business numbers banned outright. Growth here is slower and built on the list you already have plus organic visibility, not a shortcut around either.

## A realistic weekly routine (~30–45 min/day)

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Daily | Reply to any inbound message (WhatsApp, Facebook, directories) same day — this is the single highest-value habit on this whole page. |
| 2×/week | Broadcast a stock/price update to your two WhatsApp lists and post it to your Channel. |
| 2×/week | Post one short video (stock arrival, price comparison, or a quick "who we are" clip). |
| Weekly | Post an update in 2–3 relevant Facebook trade groups. |
| Monthly | Review which channel actually produced new orders (from your one-line notes in 3.3) and drop what isn't working. |
